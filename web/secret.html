How it works

At every word, the model ranks what could come next. When its top 2 to 4 choices are all reasonable and clearly ahead of the rest, the writer picks one of them to encode part of the secret; otherwise it just writes naturally. The reader runs the same model on the same text, sees the same choices, and reads the digits back.

GPUs don't compute identical numbers, so the writer only hides digits where the decision clears every threshold by a safety margin. A checksum tells the reader whether it got the message right. This is steganography, not encryption: anyone with this page can read the secret.
