Reference loss is the average surprise (nats per token) on a fixed paragraph of easy facts. Lower is smarter. Damage works in chunks of 128 weights, the unit that shares one scale factor.

Knock out each block on its own, put it back, and measure how much the reference loss rises. Tall bars are the blocks the model can't live without. Results also light up the 3D brain.
