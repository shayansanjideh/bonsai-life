What you're looking at

The dots

Each layer of Bonsai has an MLP with 6,144 neurons. Showing all 147,456 would be a fog, so each layer shows the 192 that vary most on a short warm-up text. Their activations are copied off the GPU after every layer, for every word.

Where they sit

Height is the layer. Around each ring, a neuron's angle comes from which warm-up words make it fire (the first two principal components of its firing pattern), so neurons with similar tastes line up, even across layers. Distance from the centre is spread evenly so every dot is visible.

Colours, lines and the path

Colour is how far a neuron is from its own usual level on this word (red: 3+ standard deviations above). Lines join each neuron to the neuron in the next layer it most often fires with. They show correlation, not literal wiring. The red path follows the centre of the strongest firing in each layer.
